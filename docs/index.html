About Us

Learn. Build. Question. Connect.

For students to learn practical skills and be genuinely enthusiastic about blockchain systems. To engage students to think critically about the intersection of technology, business, art, law, engineering, and science. To connect students with their local communities and to bring people together.

01

Education first

Make complex technology understandable and useful.

Ethical by design

Explore both the opportunities and tradeoffs of emerging systems.

03

Cross-disciplinary

Connect technical ideas with business, policy, culture, and society.
